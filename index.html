Calculation

How these metrics are produced

True positive

A case labelled malicious is flagged at moderate or above.

False positive

A benign case is flagged at moderate or above.

False negative

A malicious case scores below the flag threshold.

Precision

TP ÷ (TP + FP). Of everything flagged, how much was malicious.

Recall

TP ÷ (TP + FN). Of known malicious cases, how many were caught.

F1 score

Harmonic mean of precision and recall.

Security & privacy centre

Settings & privacy

Understand exactly what Sentinel stores, what requires network access, and how to remove local data.

Privacy model

Local-first by architecture

On-device
Analysis stays in your browser

URL, message and image checks run with local JavaScript. Uploaded files are read with browser APIs and are never executed.

No external reputation service

Sentinel does not call VirusTotal, WHOIS, DNS, certificate transparency or any other third-party service. Unknown reputation is shown as UNKNOWN.

Links are not opened

A scanned URL is parsed as text. Sentinel never navigates to it, requests it, or resolves its hostname.
